This browser isn’t supported

tabcat is a Chrome extension, so it works in desktop Chromium browsers that install Chrome extensions, such as Chrome, Edge, Brave, Whale, Opera and Vivaldi. Phone and tablet browsers, Samsung Internet, Firefox and Safari can’t run it.

Please use tabcat in a supported browser.

tabcat extension not detected

  1. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick a checkout of the tabcat repository (main branch).
  2. Make sure this page's origin () is listed in content_scripts.matches and web_accessible_resources.matches of the extension's manifest.json.
  3. Reload this page.

      Help

      How things are organized

      Workspace › Category › Group › Folder (up to 4 levels) and Link. Pick a workspace on the left and a category in the bar above the board; groups are the cards on the board.

      Saving tabs

      • Drag a tab from Open tabs onto a group, a folder or between links. Shift-click or Shift-drag to pick several tabs (across windows), then drag one to bring them all, or right-click for Save / Suspend / Move to a new window / Close.
      • Drag a window's header to save every tab in it; drop on the gap between groups or below a column to make a new group.
      • Toolbar button: rename the tab and pick any group or folder under Save to.
      • Saved tabs close when Close tabs after saving is on (Settings).

      The board

      • Groups stack in columns; the number of columns is in the category bar and in Settings.
      • Drag a group by its header: drop on a card's top or bottom edge, a gap, or the space below a column to place it; drop on the middle of another group to merge them.
      • Hover a gap between groups and click the line, or hover below a column, to add a group there.
      • Click empty space in a group's header to collapse or expand it; double-click its name to edit.

      Selecting groups

      • Drag a box over empty board space, or Ctrl/⌘-click a header. Shift inverts, Ctrl/⌘ adds.
      • While groups are selected, clicks select instead of opening links. Drag one to move them all; Delete removes them; Esc clears.
      • Links and folders: Shift-click to select; selection stays within the first item's group. Right-click a selected item for actions on all of them.
      • Shift-drag a box anywhere: over two or more groups, a group's header or most of a group it selects groups; within one group it selects that group's links and folders.

      Notes and symbols

      • Group notes are Markdown with task checkboxes and math: $…$ or \(…\) inline, $$…$$ or \[…\] on its own, and LaTeX environments such as \begin{align}…\end{align} or \begin{pmatrix}…\end{pmatrix}.
      • A symbol is one emoji, letter or digit; search emoji by name (in English) in the editor.

      Memory

      • Suspend unloads inactive tabs (they reload when opened); Dedupe closes duplicate tabs.
      • Auto-suspend after N idle minutes is in Settings.
